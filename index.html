WHAT IS PILATES

In an individual Pilates session, you can expect a tailored and whole-body approach to movement. One that emphasizes building a stable foundation, improving posture, and increasing overall flexibility and mobility. Each session will be customized to address specific goals while considering individual movement patterns, injuries, and general aches and pains.

During a session, you will be led through a series of carefully designed and choreographed mat-based exercises, along with utilizing Pilates equipment like the Reformer, Cadillac, and Wunda Chair. You will learn to incorporate breathing techniques to build overall body awareness.

Step-by-step guidance, instruction and hands-on adjustments are constant throughout the practice to ensure proper alignment and muscle engagement. Maintaining the integrity of movement leads to a strong foundation and optimal results.
