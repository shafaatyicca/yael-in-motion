My approach

I am here for you however you choose to identify. I love teaching all shapes, abilities, ages, prenatal and postnatal. I support all BIPOC and LGBTQIA+ people and all other humans.

While I am classically trained in Pilates, I have studied many other modalities and love to integrate them. I use bands, small props and light weights with breathwork for a whole-body experience. I will teach you patience and intuition, and always scale movement to your goal. My mission is to build trust between us, and to teach you to trust yourself.

I craft exercise plans that help you move better in our sessions, but more importantly in life. I will listen and put your thoughts first. This is your practice, and we do it together.

My goal is to help you feel confident and strong in your movement. I love people and I love movement, and creating a space where both come together is my passion.
