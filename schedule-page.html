Good to know

A few simple policies that keep things fair for everyone.

Running late

If you are going to be late and can text me, please do. I have no problem with late arrivals, but I cannot extend your session. It will finish at the scheduled time, so you will get a shorter lesson.

If I need to cancel

I am the primary caregiver for two young humans. Very infrequently, things come up that are beyond my control. I will always give you notice and do everything I can to reschedule you that same week.

Cancellations

Please give me 24 hours notice. I know life happens, but please respect my time and my other clients.

  • Under 24 hours notice Half of the session fee
  • Repeated (2x) Full cost of the missed sessions
