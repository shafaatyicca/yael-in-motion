Pricing

Sessions are 55 minutes and are $95 a session. I do not offer packages. I am happy to travel to your home for $125 a session.

Tardiness

If you are going to be late and are able to text me, please do. I have no problem if you are late, but cannot extend your session and will finish at the scheduled time. As in, you will get a shorter lesson.

My End

I am the primary caregiver for two young humans. Very infrequently, things come up in my life that are beyond my control. I will always give you notice if I have to cancel and will do everything I can to reschedule you that same week.

Cancellations

Please give me 24 hour notice if you’re going to cancel. I know life happens, but please be respectful of my time and other clients. If this becomes a recurring problem (2x) I will charge you for the cost of the entire session(s) missed. If you are unable to give me more than 24 hours notice, I will charge you for half of the session.
