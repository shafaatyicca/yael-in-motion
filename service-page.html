Pre and postnatal

I practiced Pilates throughout my three pregnancies and love teaching birthing parents during this time.

After three babies in three years, my body needed time and focus to heal, and Pilates got me there. After pregnancy and birth, your body needs gentle, low-impact movement that builds strength gradually.

We focus on basic movements that help you understand your core and pelvic floor after childbirth, whether you had a c-section or a vaginal delivery.

Dynamic movement

Dynamic movements use a range of motion across several joints and muscle groups, performed with control and fluidity. They build strength, mobility and range of motion, and support athletic performance.

I build them into your session with resistance bands, bodyweight exercises and light weights, to develop full-body awareness.

Injury prevention

Pilates improves overall physical function. It is an excellent way to prevent or rehabilitate injury, for regular exercisers and for anyone who struggles with pain.

  • Improved strength
  • Better balance
  • Increased mobility
  • Body awareness
  • Less physical stress
  • Calmer nervous system
  • Mental and emotional health

Stability and mobility training

Some joints are stiff while others are loose, so it matters which muscles perform which actions. Stability training creates a steady foundation. Mobility training improves range of motion and reduces stiffness or pain, and it is a core part of Pilates.
