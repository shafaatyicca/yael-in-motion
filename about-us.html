About Yael-In-Motion

A life in movement, now in your practice

Hi, I’m Yael Japha Trainer.

I have been on the move since I was born. I express all of myself, my thoughts, my goals, my sadness and my joy, through moving my body.

I was a dancer from the age of 2 until I turned 30. Over the years I managed and owned a number of Pilates studios. Through all of them I came to one conclusion: the best way to deliver the full experience of my knowledge is an independent studio practice.

It is a place where I can integrate every area and concept of Pilates into a truly individual, tailored program for you.

My journey

From the dance studio to my own Pilates practice.

Age 2 to 30

Age 25

2009

Returned home to Denver and joined Pilates Aligned as a teacher, while training for certification through The Pilates Center of Boulder.

Early teaching years

Graduated from the Kathy Grant Heritage Training under Cara Reeser, and became certified to teach children.

After managing and owning several studios, I teach in my own independent studio practice.
